# Vaporwave

## Palette
Core (named + hex — a tight, high-glow set; drop any that feel off):
- `hotline pink #FF6AD5`
- `laser cyan #05FFA1`
- `electric blue #01CDFE`
- `ultraviolet #B967FF`
- `sun yellow #FFFB96`
Gradient anchors (for skies / backdrops): sunset `#FF6AD5 → #FEC9C9 → #FFFB96`, dusk `#2D1B4E → #B967FF → #01CDFE`.
Base neutrals: `deep indigo #1A1030`, `off-white #F4F0FF`.

Forbidden: naturalistic greens/browns and earth tones; pure alarm red `#FF0000`; muddy desaturated pastels with no glow; flat corporate "material-design" colors.

## Motifs — use
- Neon perspective grid receding to a low horizon
- Sunset/gradient skies with a sun (solid or scanline-striped)
- Chrome / glossy-3D lettering and objects
- Generic, non-identifiable Greco-Roman busts & marble (see `ip-blocklist.md`)
- Retro tech hardware — CRT monitors, beige PCs, cassettes, floppy disks (ties into the **battlestation** niche: desk setups, RGB, old-PC-on-a-grid)
- Early-web / OS-chrome UI frames, dialog boxes, pixel cursors (original, not real brand UI)
- Palm silhouettes, checkerboard floors, dolphins, statues on pedestals
- Texture layer: VHS tracking lines, CRT scanlines, light chromatic aberration

## Motifs — avoid
- Fullwidth "ａｅｓｔｈｅｔｉｃ" text slapped on with no design intent
- The one over-used Helios/David bust everyone recycles — pick less-seen forms
- Direct copies or near-copies of famous album art (e.g. Floral Shoppe)
- Real brand UI (Windows/Mac chrome, logos) — IP, and a tell of low effort
- Lo-fi anime-girl crops (IP risk + exhausted cliché)
- A bare stock gradient with a centered word and nothing else

## Composition
- One strong focal subject; let the gradient/negative space breathe around it
- Central or mirror-symmetric placement; horizon low when using the grid
- High contrast with intentional bloom/glow on light sources
- Always a subtle texture pass (grain + scanlines) so it doesn't read as flat vector
- Target square `1:1` or `4:5` for marketplace thumbnails
