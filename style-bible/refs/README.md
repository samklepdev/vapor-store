# Private reference anchors (gitignored)

5–10 A-grade exemplar images per aesthetic that you judge "signature." They are
**private taste anchors** the future pre-QC step scores drafts against — **not**
for redistribution. This repo is **public**, so everything under
`style-bible/refs/` (except this README) is **gitignored** and never committed.

## Layout (local only)
    style-bible/refs/vaporwave/     # drop your vaporwave anchors here
    style-bible/refs/psychedelia/   # drop your psychedelia anchors here

The `## Reference set` section of `style-bible/vaporwave.md` / `psychedelia.md`
then lists the *paths* (e.g. `style-bible/refs/vaporwave/01.png`). The paths are
committed; the image files stay on your machine.

## Commercial-use note
These are personal reference/inspiration only. If a later step feeds them to a
vision model (pre-QC) or — especially — trains a sellable style LoRA (C-phase),
revisit: do not train a commercial model on others' copyrighted images. Prefer
your own work as anchors where you can.
