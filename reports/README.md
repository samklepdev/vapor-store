# Market-signal tracker (M1)

The point of M1 is **signal, not efficiency**. Each week, spend 5 minutes reading
your Gumroad (and any other channel) stats and logging them. This is what drives
the weekly go/no-go — and first sales + repeat interest here is the trigger that
unlocks M2 automation.

## How to use
1. Copy `signal-log.template.csv` → `signal-log.csv` (already done; it's gitignored
   so your real numbers stay private on this public repo).
2. Each Monday, add a row per product/channel: views, favorites, sales, revenue, a note.
3. Read the signals below; decide what to kill and what to fuel.

## Reading the signals (from the design spec §6)
| Signal | Timeframe | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Views / impressions coming in | 1–3 wks | People are finding it. Low views → SEO/discovery problem (fix tags/pins), not the art. |
| Favorites but no sales | 2–3 wks | Appealing but friction — price, cover/mockup, or bundle is off. |
| **First sales + repeat views on a theme** | 3–4+ wks | **Winner — double down.** This is the M2 trigger. |
| Flat views *and* favorites | ~3 wks | Deprioritize — wrong niche or aesthetic-market fit. |

## Weekly go/no-go ritual
- **Kill** the flat (deprioritize, stop promoting).
- **Fuel** the winner (more of that theme, more promotion, a bundle).
- **Note** which specific designs/themes move — that steers the next batch.

Expectation: first sales commonly take 2–6 weeks. Don't over-react to week 1.
